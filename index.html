  3. Have a display cookie — your showstopper, left whole for admiring.

🍪 Baker's Cookie Gallery

Need a little inspiration before the ovens go on? From iced sugar cookies to peanut butter criss-crosses and proper gingerbread men, here are some classics to get your oven mitts twitching.

Iced Sugar Cookies

Signature bake

Buttery cut-outs flooded in royal icing — Santas, twinkly trees, ugly sweaters and baubles.

Chocolate-Dipped Peanut Butter Cookies

Technical challenge

Classic fork criss-cross peanut butter cookies, half-dipped in chocolate and showered with crushed candy cane and festive sprinkles.

Iced Gingerbread

Showstopper

Treacle-dark gingerbread snowflakes, reindeer, trees and gingerbread men piped with snowy icing.

Melted Snowman Cookies

Signature bake

Buttery cookies with a puddle of white icing and a cheeky snowman peeking out in his green scarf.

Snowy Chocolate Crinkles

Showstopper

Fudgy cocoa cookies that crack open under a snowfall of icing sugar.

Candy Cane Kiss Cookies

Signature bake

Soft sugar cookies rolled in festive sprinkles with a candy-cane striped kiss pressed in the centre.
